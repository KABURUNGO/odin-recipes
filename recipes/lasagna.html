Lasagna

Description

This is a classic homemade lasagna recipe with layers of meat sauce, cheese, and pasta. It is hearty and perfect for family dinner.

It takes about 1 hour to make and serves 6 people. Even if you have never cooked lasagna before, you can follow these simple steps.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500g minced beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Boil lasagna noodles in salted water for 8 minutes, then drain.
  3. Heat oil in a pan, fry onion and garlic, then add minced beef and cook until brown.
  4. Add tomato sauce, salt and pepper, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses. Repeat 3 times.
  6. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  7. Remove foil and bake for another 10 minutes until cheese is golden.
  8. Let it cool for 10 minutes, then serve.
