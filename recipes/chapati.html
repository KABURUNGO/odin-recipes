Soft Chapati

Description

Soft, layered Kenyan chapati is a staple in every home. Best served with beans, ndengu, or tea. My mother taught me to roll it with oil for softness.

Takes 40 minutes, makes 8 chapatis. The trick is hot water and resting the dough.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups wheat flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil + extra for frying

Steps

  1. Mix flour and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add oil and mix, then add warm water little by little and knead to soft dough.
  3. Cover dough and rest for 20 minutes.
  4. Divide into 8 balls, roll each into circle, brush oil, fold, and roll again.
  5. Heat pan on medium heat.
  6. Place chapati, cook 30 seconds, flip, brush oil, flip again until golden spots appear.
  7. Repeat for all, keep in container to stay soft.
