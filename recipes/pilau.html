Kenyan Pilau

Description

Pilau is a famous Swahili spiced rice dish from the Coast. Fragrant, flavorful, and loved during celebrations. My family makes it every Sunday.

It takes 45 minutes and serves 4 people. The secret is the pilau masala and not stirring too much.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g beef, cubed
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp pilau masala
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 4 cups water or beef broth
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Wash rice and soak for 15 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat oil, fry onions until dark brown (not burnt).
  3. Add garlic, beef, pilau masala, cumin, and salt. Fry until beef browns.
  4. Add water/broth and bring to boil.
  5. Add rice, stir once, cover with tight lid.
  6. Cook on low heat for 20 minutes until water dries.
  7. Turn off heat, let rest 5 minutes, then serve with kachumbari.
